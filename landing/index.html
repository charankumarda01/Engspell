🇮🇳 Trained for Indian Voices: Accent Neutralization

Master clean, globally intelligible international English without erasing your identity. Designed specifically around Indian-English phonological patterns.

👄

V vs W Allophone Contrast

Indian tongues often switch /v/ and /w/, turning "very well" into "wery vell". EngSpell's Ear & Voice drills train:

  • /v/ friction: upper teeth on inner lower lip (vine, vet, vest, viper)
  • /w/ rounding: lip-circle glide with zero teeth contact (wine, wet, west, wiper)
  • Instant word-level detection of the "desi twin" when speaking
👅

Dental TH vs Hard T/D Stops

Replacing English dental fricatives with plosives turns "think" into "tink" and "this" into "dis":

  • Tongue between teeth: soft continuous airflow without stopping
  • Voiceless /θ/: think, thank, thick, three, thin
  • Voiced /ð/: this, that, there, they, breathe
🎵

Stress-Timed Rhythm & Chunking

Indian languages give equal syllable duration (syllable-timed), while English relies on stress-timing:

  • Word stress: de-VEL-op (not DE-ve-lop), COM-fer-t'bl (3 syllables)
  • Function word reduction: compress "to the", "and a", "of our" into quick schwa (/ə/)
  • Wave-like cadence across 12 graded sentence drills

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EngSpell.

Is EngSpell really 100% free forever?

Yes. There are no subscriptions, no locked premium tiers, no credit cards, and no ads. All core curriculum, exercises, phonetic audio, and local AI engines are open and free forever.

Does EngSpell require an internet connection?

No. EngSpell is designed offline-first. All lessons, flashcards, word banks, and browser-native speech recognition run entirely on your device without pinging an external server.

How does the live speech interrupt engine work?

Using the browser's native Web Speech API, EngSpell performs client-side word-by-word alignment as you speak. If you hesitate for too long or repeat filler sounds, the coach gently pauses you so you can restart with clean cadence.

What is Nova AI Coach and do I need an API key?

Nova is our conversational speaking partner. It features a built-in offline rule engine that requires zero setup. For deeper generative conversation, you can optionally paste your free Google Gemini API key in Settings.

Can EngSpell fix or neutralize my Indian accent?

Yes! EngSpell's Indian Accent Studio features 7 research-backed packs specifically targeting Indian English phonology: V vs W, dental TH vs T/D, rhotic R vs tap/roll, alveolar vs retroflex T/D, word stress timing, diphthongs (ship vs sheep, go vs say), and sentence rhythm. You train your ear with Listen Quizzes, verify your speech with word-level Voice Drills, and unlock international confidence 100% offline.
